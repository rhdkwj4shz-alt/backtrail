Press kit

Backtrail is a free app for iPhone and Apple Watch that helps walkers, paddlers and boaters when something goes wrong with no signal.

Short description

Backtrail helps when something goes wrong outdoors with no signal. It shows your grid reference to read to rescuers, coaches CPR on your phone or wrist, and takes you back along your own track to the car. Everything for an emergency is free, with no account. Backtrail Plus, one payment, adds saved offline topo maps, route following and planning.

Longer description

Backtrail is a safety app for iPhone and Apple Watch, built in Australia by one developer. Its home keeps Call in the same place, with two doors, Someone's hurt and Get help, so help is a tap or two away. With no signal, it gives your grid reference and reads it aloud, keeps a CPR coach with a beat on your wrist, sounds Six Blasts, and holds about 105 offline guides to first aid, survival, water and boating. Backtrack records your walk, and Take me back follows it to the car. On Apple Watch, I'm lost points an arrow to your car, your pack or your friends. Everything for an emergency is free for good. Backtrail Plus is one payment with no subscription, shared with up to six people in a family.

Free, for good

  • The SOS home, with Call 000, 111, 112, 999 or 911
  • The CPR coach on iPhone and Apple Watch
  • About 105 offline guides
  • Grid references to read to rescuers, read aloud
  • Signals: Six Blasts, a torch SOS and a flashing screen
  • The "Are you OK?" timer
  • Follow-ups after a hard fall, or a fall into water, on Apple Watch
  • Backtrack and Take me back to the car
  • I'm lost on Apple Watch: an arrow back to the car, your pack or your friends
  • Find Each Other: phone to phone, nearby, with no signal
  • Medical ID and a Safety Check-up

What Backtrail Plus adds

  • Saving offline topo maps, and maps for Apple Watch
  • Following GPX and KML routes
  • Planning: turn-back times, forecasts and kit lists
  • Activity tools: tides, an anchor alarm, avalanche forecasts and a slope meter
  • Starting a group
  • Learn, with practice questions
  • iCloud backup and saving walks to Health

What Backtrail doesn't do

  • It doesn't call or message anyone by itself. Call hands over to the phone's normal emergency call.
  • It doesn't send your location to the emergency services or anyone else. Messages you choose to send include your position.
  • It doesn't send anything by satellite. Emergency SOS via satellite is Apple's own feature, on iPhone 14 or later.
  • It doesn't replace a personal locator beacon, a map and compass, Emergency+, a trip intention form or a satellite messenger.
  • It's a memory aid. It isn't a medical device, and it doesn't replace first aid training.
